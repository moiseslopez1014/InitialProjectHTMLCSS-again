    The website will consist of:

    1. Header section with a company logo, a nav with links. and another nav with a search button and a telephone number (clickable)
    2. Main section with a stunning cover with restaurant description of services; statistics of the company and site; about the restaurant and purpose; form to Q&A; and a form to subscription to newsletter.
    3. Footer section with another company logo and text, three navs with links, and a copyright in the bottom of the page.
